Task 4.4.2.i. Digit Sum.

Write a function called digitSum() that returns the sum of the digits of a positive integer n.
🔗
Use a while-loop that performs the following for each iteration:
  • Extract the rightmost digit as the remainder from dividing by 10.
    🔗
  • Add that digit to a running total sum.
    🔗
  • Remove the rightmost digit by dividing by 10 and rounding down.
    🔗
🔗
Inputs:
n
🔗
(1x1) double
🔗
positive integer
🔗
Outputs:
s
🔗
(1x1) double
🔗
sum of the digits of n
🔗
Test Cases ⤵︎
🔗
s = digitSum(123)
s = digitSum(9999)
s = digitSum(7)
s = digitSum(10001)
Expected ⤵︎
🔗
s = 6
s = 36
s = 7
s = 2
