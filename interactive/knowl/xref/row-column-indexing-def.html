👀 Quick Review 6.28. Row/Column Indexing.

🔗
🔗
To access the value in row \(\texttt{i}\) and column \(\texttt{j}\) of matrix \(\texttt{A}\) , use the command:
🔗
					A(i,j)

📝
Important:
  • the row and column index must be separated by a comma, and
    🔗
  • rows are indexed first.
    🔗
🔗
Visually, the command, \(\texttt{A(i,j)}\,\,\) , will give you the value located at \(\boxed{*}\) .
🔗
\(\begin{matrix} \amp \amp \quad\texttt{j} \amp \\ \amp \amp \quad\downarrow \amp \end{matrix}\)
\(\begin{matrix} \\ \\ \texttt{i} \rightarrow \end{matrix}\) \(\left[ \begin{matrix} \amp \amp \vdots \amp \\ \amp \amp \vdots \amp \\ \dots \amp \dots \amp \boxed{*} \amp \dots \\ \amp \amp \vdots \amp \end{matrix} \right]\)
